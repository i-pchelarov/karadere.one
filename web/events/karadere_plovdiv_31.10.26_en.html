Why

Some places hold a piece of who we are. Karadere is one of them: tents under the stars, campfires, music, strangers becoming friends, conversations until sunrise.

But what happens if one day we return and all of this is forbidden? We don't want to wait for that day. Wild camping is a right passed down from our ancestors, and it must remain ours.

On the eve of National Awakeners' Day, we gather not to protest, but to see each other, listen to one another, and to turn the Karadere community into something bigger: a community that is awake, connected, and ready to act.

What to Expect

No set schedule, no strict script. Bring whatever you have:

  • Talent and good vibes: music, drawing, henna, braids, massage, Tarot reading, or simply good conversation
  • Shared Table: homemade food prepared with love in support of the cause
  • Charity Bazaar: clothes, camping gear, and items looking for a new life

Come with your friends, bring an instrument, an idea, or just yourself. Because nature doesn't protect itself. And because we are its voice.

Together for Karadere. So we always have a place to return to.
